ATR Volatility Position Sizing

Individual position sizes are normalized against 14-day Average True Range (ATR) so high-volatility assets receive proportionally smaller capital commitments.

Parameter 02

Option Underwriting Guardrails

Short put and call legs are initiated primarily within the 0.15 to 0.30 Delta band with 30–45 DTE, targeting optimal Theta decay and a statistical cushion.

Target POP: 70% – 85% Probability
