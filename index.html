Risk Protocol 01

ATR Volatility Position Sizing

Every position is dynamically sized using Average True Range (ATR) volatility metrics so higher-volatility assets receive smaller capital weights, equalizing risk across the portfolio.

Single-Trade Risk Cap: ≤ 1.5% of Portfolio NAV
Risk Protocol 02

RSI & Bollinger Regime Filters

Quantitative screening models evaluate multi-timeframe Relative Strength Index (RSI), Bollinger Band extremes, and 200-day moving average support before initiating option underwriting.

Target Option Delta Band: 0.15 – 0.30 Delta (30–45 DTE)
Risk Protocol 03

Strict Drawdown & Exit Guardrails

Pre-defined profit-taking targets (50%–75% max credit capture) and mechanical rolling/exit rules prevent isolated derivative positions from compounding into portfolio-level drawdowns.

Execution Cadence: Automated End-of-Day Telemetry
